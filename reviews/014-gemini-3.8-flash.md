## Critique of Step 013 (Cyber-Telemetry Terminal RunCard)

Step 013 implemented a high-contrast UNIX shell command-prompt console (`agent@benchmark:~$ run-card`), featuring a live telemetry ticker, CAD grid reticle canvas stage, and multi-buffer pure CSS tabs.

### Strengths
- **DevOps Authenticity**: The shell prompt header and deep-ink surfaces communicate raw engineering capability.
- **CAD Reticle Precision**: The technical grid framing the 600x600 SVG artwork emphasizes coordinate accuracy and vector standards.
- **Buffer Segmentation**: Isolating the canvas, deliberation trace, and BYOK specs into discrete buffers preserves clean organization.

### Weaknesses & Evolution Opportunities
1. **Holistic Model Profiling**: While the terminal presents linear metrics, comprehensive AI evaluation requires a multi-dimensional diagnostic profile (balancing generation speed, reasoning depth, instruction fidelity, and economic efficiency).
2. **Radar & Constellation Telemetry**: Modern AI benchmarking dashboards often benefit from a "Radar / Constellation" layout where key performance vectors surround the central artifact like satellites.
3. **Multi-Vector Assessment**: Integrating quantitative scorecards directly into the primary overview allows evaluators to compare models across multiple operational axes at a single glance.
