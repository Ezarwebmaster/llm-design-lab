## Critique of Step 009 (Dual-Lens Benchmark Inspector)

Step 009 created a split-mode analytical workbench juxtaposing prompt intent and cognitive steps on the left against the synthesized SVG vector canvas on the right, supported by floating telemetry beads (`105.4 tok/s`, `0.88s TTFT`, `25.0s`, `$0.0014`).

### Strengths
- **Analytical Juxtaposition**: Placing the prompt's linguistic requirements directly alongside the rendered SVG artwork provides instant verification of instruction-following fidelity.
- **Floating Telemetry Beads**: Granular separation of throughput, wall time, TTFT, and BYOK cost into dedicated compact beads improves scan speed.
- **Cognitive Flow Structure**: Numbered deliberation steps clearly communicate how the model planned color palettes and spatial geometry.

### Weaknesses & Evolution Opportunities
1. **Milestone Synthesis**: As Step 010 represents a major evolutionary milestone in the benchmark lab, it should synthesize the best elements of previous paradigms: the density of the Linear HUD, the depth of the observability cockpit, and the beauty of the immersive canvas.
2. **Telemetry Micro-Matrix**: Consolidating performance indicators into an integrated "Mission Control" telemetry deck that gives engineers instant access to generation velocity, latency percentiles, and token cost without clutter.
3. **Execution Diagnostics**: Providing a native, pure-CSS multi-lens switch between the production artwork showcase, full neural deliberation terminal, and the complete financial invoice.

## Design Objective for Step 010: Mission Control Benchmark Suite

Step 010 introduces the **Mission Control Benchmark Suite**:
- **Flagship Mission Control Header**: Precision flight-deck status beacon, provider identity lockup (`openai/gpt-6-luna`), and real-time generation velocity badge (`105.4 tok/s`).
- **Telemetry HUD Matrix**: Integrated 4-quadrant precision readouts for throughput, time-to-first-token (`0.88s`), streaming latency (`24.12s`), and token composition.
- **Multi-Lens Inspection Deck**: Pure CSS radio tabs toggling between:
  - *Studio Canvas*: Showcase frame for the 600x600 sunset SVG illustration with direct view mode toggles and asset export.
  - *Cognitive Terminal*: Monospace deep-ink terminal breaking down the 1,008 reasoning tokens into structured execution phases.
  - *Economics & Ledger*: Complete BYOK pricing audit ($0.0014 total, $0.10/M input, $0.50/M output).
- **100% Token Compliance**: Fully tokenized implementation using `system/tokens.css` with fluid mobile responsiveness.
