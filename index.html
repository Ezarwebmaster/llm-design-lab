## Critique of Step 011 (Tactile Bottom-Sheet RunCard) Step 011 embraced mobile-first design, featuring a full-bleed top canvas stage and an interactive tactile bottom sheet with visual grab handle and swipeable horizontal telemetry chips. ### Strengths - **Mobile Thumb Ergonomics**: Drag-handle drawer and swipeable telemetry stream solve narrow screen navigation naturally. - **Full-Bleed Visual Impact**: The 600x600 SVG artwork receives maximum screen real estate without crowded boundaries. - **Telemetry Chips**: Compact chips give immediate glanceable throughput (105.4 tok/s) and latency (25.0s). ### Weaknesses & Evolution Opportunities 1. **Desktop Expansion**: While responsive, bottom-sheet patterns are inherently optimized for mobile and feel less commanding on ultra-wide desktop monitors. 2. **Scientific Precision & Laboratory Tone**: High-throughput AI researchers and prompt engineers often prefer a laboratory instrument feel—a "Prism Spectrum" aesthetic that maps tokens and latency as continuous scientific bands. 3. **Multi-Band Diagnostic Matrix**: Synthesizing the visual spectrum with direct deep-trace reasoning tabs creates a harmonious balance of beauty and empirical measurement. ## Design Objective for Step 012: Prism Multi-Spectrum Benchmark Card Step 012 introduces the **Prism Multi-Spectrum Benchmark Card**: - **Laboratory Instrument Aesthetic**: Refined scientific framing with monochromatic slate surfaces accented by semantic telemetry badges (`var(--color-mint)`, `var(--color-purple-custom)`, `var(--color-pink-custom)`). - **Prism Spectrum Meter**: Continuous multi-band photon bar mapping the 2,999 tokens across prompt (364), reasoning (1,008), and generated vectors (1,627). - **Multi-Band Diagnostics**: Pure CSS radio tabs toggling between Prism Visual Studio, Neural Reasoning Spectrum, and BYOK Financial Ledger. - **100% Token Compliance**: Fully tokenized styling relying exclusively on custom properties from `system/tokens.css`.
